Small, sharp apps for specific jobs.

Most of these exist because somebody was doing a precise, repetitive job out of a notebook — an inspector recording kVp readings, a game master resetting a room between bookings, a piper trying to remember which reed sang last Saturday. The apps are narrow on purpose, they say plainly what they cannot do, and most of them work with no signal at all.
