Setup

Added in seconds

Discover miners on the LAN, type a host and port, or scan the QR code of your payout address to pull in pool stats.

Alerts, honestly

Set your own thresholds; the app watches them and tells you exactly when it can watch them.

Runs hot

Temperature ceiling

An alert when a device crosses the temperature you set, before the chip throttles itself.

Falls off

Hashrate floor

An alert when output drops under your floor — the first sign of a bad fan, a bad PSU, or a bad flash.

Goes dark

Offline

An alert when a miner stops answering at all.

These alerts need the app open

Device alerts fire while Miner Monitor is running, because your miners live on your LAN and nothing else can reach them. Push alerts for pool stats that work with the app closed are the next release.

It is a monitor, not a manager

Miner Monitor reads your devices and restarts them. It does not tune, overclock, flash firmware, or configure your pool.
