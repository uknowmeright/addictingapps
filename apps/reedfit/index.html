What ReedFit will not do

A reed log is only worth keeping if you can trust what it tells you. So we are precise about the limits.

It does not listen to your pipes

No microphone, no acoustic analysis, no diagnosis. ReedFit records what you enter and reports arithmetic over it.

It does not guess at patterns

No trend or condition language appears until a setup has at least three tests, and every claim states its sample size.

It does not rewrite history

Once a setup has been tested it is locked. Editing it records a new snapshot, so old tests always belong to the setup they were recorded on.

It does not pick for you

Ready backups are your call. ReedFit only asks that the reed is active and the setup has a test of its own.

Yours, on your phone

No account. No analytics. No ads. No server of ours. Nothing to sign into and nothing to leak.
